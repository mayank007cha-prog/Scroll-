The last frame is the first frame

The ball on your screen is the hero ball: same size, same spot, same bounce phase. The push-in ends on a contact at f285, the screen glass dissolves, and the ball rises to the apex where f0 starts. No fade, no cut.

Rethinks

What I changed from the brief

One surface for everything

The ball bounces on the same plane your hand rests on. When the camera pulls out, it turns out the ball was bouncing on your desk the whole time, so shots 1 to 4 feel like one space.

The ball goes flat as it becomes a dot

The shaded 3D pearl loses its lighting and turns into a flat dot. Rendered animation to flat UI in one move, which is your career shift in half a second.

Each dot becomes a fingernail

Instead of fingers appearing next to the dots, each dot grows into a fingertip and the loader wave becomes fingers drumming on the desk. Loading and thinking share one beat.

The screen is the seam

The screen ball is phase-locked to the hero ball, so the push-in lands exactly on frame 0. The loop never visibly restarts.

How the final loop gets made

Two kinds of layers: the graphic ones are drawn in code on the page, and the shots with you are footage with the background removed.

Before I build the page

What I need from you

  • Approval of the timing. Scrub the animatic. Tell me any shot that feels too fast or too slow; the total can grow past 12.5 s in 1.25 s steps.
  • Route for shots 3–4. (a) You film yourself on a phone against a plain wall and I key it out, (b) footage generated from your photos with an image-to-video tool you have access to, or (c) keep it illustrated.
  • 4–6 photos of you if we go with (a) or (b): front, both three-quarter sides, profile, and back of head over the shoulder. Same glasses and shirt you want in the video, even light.
  • 2 photos of your right hand resting on a table, fingertips down like your reference: one from the front at table height, one from above.
  • Dot color. Neutral pearl as shown, or one brand accent. All four dots stay the same color either way.
  • Fold layout and copy. Animation on the right with the headline left, or centered with the headline below. Plus your name, nav links and the main call to action.
