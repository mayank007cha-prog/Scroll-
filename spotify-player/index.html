The floating player

A mini player that drifts, docks and tucks itself away, so your music stays close without covering what you're browsing.

  1. Drag the player anywhere. Let go and it glides to the nearest edge.
  2. Push it into the edge and it folds into a spinning disc, half tucked away.
  3. Tap the disc, or pull it inward, to bring the player back.
  4. Scroll down to hide the tab bar. Scroll up and it returns.
  5. Tap any poster to play its soundtrack. The player, disc and header take on its artwork and colour.
