The floating player

A mini player that drifts, docks and tucks itself away, so your music stays close without covering what you're browsing.

  1. Tap Koi Fariyaad to open the song page. The down arrow shrinks it into the floating player.
  2. Tap Tamasha for the music video. Minimise it and the video keeps playing in the floating player.
  3. Drag the player anywhere, or push it into the edge to tuck it away as a spinning disc. Tap the disc to bring it back.
  4. Swipe the song title left or right to skip. Tap the player to open the song page again.
  5. Scroll down to hide the tab bar. Scroll up and it returns.
