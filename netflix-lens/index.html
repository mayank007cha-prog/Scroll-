Interaction prototype

Focus Lens

Titles baked into poster art are hard to read. Every poster has its own typeface, size and contrast. Rows scroll normally. Long-press any poster and it glides to the centre, zooms in a touch and gets a small glass panel over its lower edge with the title in one clear style. The artwork stays exactly as designed.

  • ScrollDrag a row as usual. No lens, just a normal carousel.
  • HoldLong-press a poster. It glides to the centre and its title appears on the glass.
  • SlideWith the lens open, drag the row. Each poster takes its turn in the centre.
  • CloseTap anywhere outside that row and the lens goes away.
