Focus Lens

Titles baked into poster art are hard to read. Every poster has its own typeface, size and contrast. Long-press any poster, or slide a row: a glass lens opens in the centre of the carousel and the posters pass through it. Whatever sits in the lens gets its title set in one clear style on the lens floor.

  • HoldLong-press a poster. The lens opens and that poster glides into it.
  • SlideDrag a row left or right. Posters pass through the lens at the speed of your thumb.
  • ReleaseThe row glides on and the nearest poster settles in the lens.
  • PauseThree seconds without scrolling and the lens melts away.
