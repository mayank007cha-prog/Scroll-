Interaction prototype

Focus Lens

Poster art makes titles hard to read, and checking what a film is about usually means opening it and coming back. Long-press any poster for quick view: it zooms in, its title sits on a glass panel, and below it three labels say whether it is a movie or series and whether audio and subtitles exist in your display language, followed by a three-line gist. Scroll down and each row pops in turn.

  • HoldLong-press a poster to open quick view on it.
  • SlideDrag the row. Each poster takes its turn, and the labels and gist change with it.
  • ScanScroll the page. Whichever row reaches the focus line pops on its own.
  • TapIn quick view, tap any poster to bring it into focus.
  • CloseTap the round × at the bottom right, or press Esc.
  • LanguageMy Netflix → tap your profile name → pencil → Display language → English or हिन्दी. The home rows switch to posters in that language where we have them.
