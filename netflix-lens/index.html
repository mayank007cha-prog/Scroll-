Focus Lens

Poster art makes titles hard to read, and checking what a film is about usually means opening it and coming back. Tap Quick view and the poster in the centre of a row zooms in, its title sits on a glass panel and a three-line gist appears below it. Scroll down and each row pops in turn, so you can scan and compare without opening anything.

  • BrowseRows scroll normally until you turn on quick view.
  • StartTap the Quick view button (the eye) at the bottom right.
  • SlideDrag the row. Each poster takes its turn, and the gist changes with it.
  • ScanScroll the page. Whichever row reaches the focus line pops on its own.
  • TapTap any poster to bring it into focus.
  • CloseThe same button turns into a ×. Tap it, or press Esc.
