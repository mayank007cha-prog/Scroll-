Focus Lens

Titles baked into poster art are hard to read. Every poster has its own typeface, size and contrast. Hold a poster and slide sideways: a glass lens bulges under your thumb, magnifies the artwork and sets the title in one clear style. Let go and the row looks exactly as designed.

  • MousePress on any poster and drag left or right.
  • TouchPut your thumb on a poster and slide sideways.
  • ReleaseThe lens melts away and the row keeps gliding.
