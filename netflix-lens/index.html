Focus Lens

Titles baked into poster art are hard to read. Every poster has its own typeface, size and contrast. Hold a poster and slide sideways: a glass lens opens where you first touched and stays there while the posters flow through it. The title of whatever sits in the lens floats just above it in one clear style, out from under your thumb.

  • MousePress on any poster and drag left or right.
  • TouchPut your thumb on a poster and slide sideways.
  • ReleaseThe row glides on and the nearest poster settles in the lens.
  • PauseTwo seconds without scrolling and the lens melts away.
